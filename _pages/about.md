Hi, I'm **Amirhossein** — a software engineer and final-year Computer Science student at [York University's Lassonde School of Engineering](https://lassonde.yorku.ca/), graduating May 2026. I build things end-to-end across the stack, with a soft spot for clean Java/Spring Boot services and Python tooling.

Most recently I spent **12 months at [Moneris Solutions](https://www.moneris.com/)**, Canada's largest payment processor, working on backend services in Spring Boot, building a Java-based file comparison tool that cut validation time by 60%, and integrating Postman/Newman regression testing into the CI/CD pipeline. I've also shipped Kafka-backed microservices and refactored production SQL and services for performance.

I'm currently building **[Synapse](https://github.com/amir01mn)** — a Human-Centered AI Assurance Framework for **TD Bank** as my YorkU capstone — a FastAPI + NestJS + Next.js platform that scores Jira user stories against versioned AI/privacy policies in real time, served as an Atlassian Forge plugin.

Outside of school and work, you'll find me prototyping side projects (see [projects](/projects/)) or studying for an AWS cert. Feel free to [reach out](mailto:amir01mansouri@gmail.com) — I'm always open to interesting problems and new grad / SWE opportunities for 2026.
